Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

Your data stays on your phone

Player names, nicknames, photos, scores, history, voice clips and settings are saved only on your device. Padelitics has no accounts and no servers. We never receive your games or your players' details.

What we don't collect

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no location and no contacts.

Purchases

Subscriptions, single nights, the lifetime pass and offer codes are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. We never see card details. The app only learns whether a purchase is active.

Camera, microphone and photos

Used only when you choose to take a photo, record a chant or dictate a name. What you capture stays on your device unless you share it yourself.

Sharing and backups

Posters, player cards and backup files go only where you send them.

Children

Padelitics is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete games or players inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes.

Support

Restore purchases

Settings › Padelitics Pro › Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID or Google account you paid with.

Redeem a code

Tap "Have a code?" on the paywall or in Settings.

Cancel a subscription

iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Android: Play Store › Payments & subscriptions.

Move to a new phone

Settings › Back up, then restore that file on the new phone.

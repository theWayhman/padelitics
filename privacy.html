Privacy policy

Last updated 14 November 2026 (profiles)

Padelitics helps padel groups run Americano nights: rotations, scores, rankings and highlights. It has no ads and no tracking. A Padelitics profile is optional.

Your games stay on your phone

Players, nicknames, photos, scores, history, voice clips and settings are saved only on your device. We never receive your games or your players' details.

Padelitics profiles (optional)

If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive an account identifier and, if you allow it, your name and email address. We store your profile (name, nickname, username, playing side, home club and a short bio) and link it to the player you claim in a league, after that league's admin confirms it's you.

When an admin puts a league online, the league name, its player names and the results of finished games (positions, points and matches won, with dates) are stored on our server so profiles can show titles, medals and streaks across groups. Photos stay on the phone.

Other members of a league you join can see your profile and stats. League admins see a masked email when you ask to claim a player. You can edit your profile at any time, and delete your account in the app under Settings › Your profile › Delete account. Deleting removes your profile, your sign-in, your claims and any online league you created; your past results stay in other admins' leagues without your name linked.

Attendance links (optional)

If the organiser shares an attendance link for the next game, the app sends that game's date, time, venue, court cost and the first names on the list to our server, so friends can open the link and reply In, Maybe or Out. Friends who add themselves enter only a name. This data is used only to show that link page, is never sold or shared, and is deleted automatically 90 days after the planned date. If you never share a link, nothing leaves your phone.

Reminders

On iPhone, game reminders and reply alerts are local notifications created on your device. We do not use push tokens.

What we don't collect

No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no location and no contacts.

Purchases

Subscriptions, single nights and offer codes are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. We never see card details. The app only learns whether a purchase is active.

Camera, microphone and photos

Used only when you choose to take a photo, record a chant or dictate a name. What you capture stays on your device unless you share it yourself.

Sharing and backups

Posters, player cards and backup files go only where you send them. Automatic backups are saved on your own device.

Children

Padelitics is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete games or players inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. To remove an attendance link sooner, email us the link and we delete it within two working days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new version appears here.

Support

Restore Padelitics Pro

Settings › Padelitics Pro › Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you subscribed with.

Redeem a code

Tap "Have a code?" on the paywall, or Settings › Redeem a code.

Cancel a subscription

iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Padelitics. Android: Play Store › Profile › Payments & subscriptions.

Move to a new phone

Settings › Back up, then restore that file on the new phone. Padelitics Pro moves with your store account.

Contact

Email dev@gripfitnessstudio.com. We usually reply within two working days.

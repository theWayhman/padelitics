Privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026 (Open Games, player profile photo, World padel)

Padelitics helps padel groups run game nights (Americano, Mexicano, King of the Court and Team Americano): rotations, scores, rankings and highlights. It has no ads and no tracking. A Padelitics profile is optional.

Your games stay on your phone

Players, nicknames, photos, scores, history, voice clips and settings are saved only on your device. We never receive your games or your players' details.

Padelitics profiles (optional)

If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive an account identifier and, if you allow it, your name and email address. We store your profile (name, nickname, username, playing side, home club and a short bio) and link it to the player you claim in a league, after that league's admin confirms it's you.

When an admin puts a league online, the league name, its player names and the results of finished games (positions, points and matches won, with dates) are stored on our server so profiles can show titles, medals and streaks across groups. Player photos in leagues stay on the phone.

Other members of a league you join can see your profile and stats. League admins see a masked email when you ask to claim a player. You can edit your profile at any time, and delete your account in the app under Settings › Your profile › Delete account. Deleting removes your profile, your sign-in, your claims and any online league you created,; your past results stay in other admins' leagues without your name linked.

Open Games (optional)

Open Games are public game nights that signed-in players host or join. If you use them, we store your player profile (name, nickname, playing side and hand, city), whether you accepted the Open Games rules and confirmed you are an adult, the games you host (title, format, venue and its map link, date, time, price, level, spots and notes) and the games you join, and whether you showed up (your reliability band). If you add a profile photo, it is stored on our server and shown to other Open Games players; it is the only photo that leaves your phone. Other players can see your profile, photo and reliability band.

You can report a game, a player or a photo, and block a player. We store reports (who reported what, and why) to review them, normally within 24 hours, and we may hide content or suspend accounts that break the rules. Reports are deleted after 90 days. Blocks are kept until you remove them. Past games and the list of who joined them are deleted 60 days after the game date. Deleting your account removes your profile, photo, the games you host, your joins, blocks and the reports you made.

World padel

The World padel section shows the public Premier Padel schedule and links to news articles from other websites. Opening an article or a draw takes you to that website, under its own privacy policy. We do not track what you read.

Attendance links (optional)

If the organiser shares an attendance link for the next game, the app sends that game's date, time, venue, court cost and the first names on the list to our server, so friends can open the link and reply In, Maybe or Out. Friends who add themselves enter only a name. This data is used only to show that link page, is never sold or shared, and is deleted automatically 90 days after the planned date. If you never share a link, nothing leaves your phone.

Player of the Day votes (optional)

If the organiser starts a phone vote after a game, the app sends the first names of that night's players to our server so each player can open the link and vote. We store each vote (who voted for whom) until the organiser reveals the winner, and delete the vote 30 days later.

Diagnostics

To find and fix problems, the app sends error and crash reports to our server: the error message, the app version, the device type and system version, a random install number (not linked to your name or account) and short technical details such as how balanced a game's rotation was. Reports contain no player names, photos or scores. You can turn this off in Settings › Backup & data › Diagnostics. If you tap Send diagnostics, the app also sends its recent activity log (button taps and app events, again without names) and, only if you tick the box, the current game. Reports are used only to fix the app, are never sold or shared, and are deleted after 30 days.

Reminders

On iPhone and Android, game reminders and reply alerts are local notifications created on your device. To show new replies, the app checks your attendance link on our server from time to time while a game is planned. We do not use push tokens.

What we don't collect

No advertising identifiers, no tracking across apps or websites, no location and no contacts.

Purchases

Subscriptions, single nights and offer codes are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. We never see card details. The app only learns whether a purchase is active. On Android, to show the date your subscription renews or ends, the app sends the Google Play purchase token to our server, which asks Google for that date; the token is not stored.

Camera, microphone and photos

Used only when you choose to take a photo, record a chant or dictate a name. Photos and recordings stay on your device unless you share them yourself. When you dictate on iPhone, the audio is processed by Apple's speech recognition (it may be sent to Apple to turn it into text) under Apple's privacy policy; on Android it is processed by the phone's speech service. We never receive the audio.

Sharing and backups

Posters, player cards and backup files go only where you send them. Automatic backups are saved on your own device.

Children

Padelitics is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete games or players inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored. To remove an attendance link sooner, email us the link and we delete it within two working days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new version appears here.

Delete your account

Padelitics: Padel Game Nights, by BEEYOND TECH

In the app (fastest)

Open Padelitics › Settings › Your profile › Delete account, then confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

By email

If you no longer have the app, email dev@gripfitnessstudio.com from the email address of your Apple or Google sign-in with the subject "Delete my Padelitics account". We delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your profile (name, nickname, username, playing side, home club, bio), your sign-in identifiers and email, your sessions, your claims on players, and any online league you created, with its members, games and shared league state, plus your Open Games profile and photo, the games you host, your joins, blocks and the reports you made.

What is kept

Results of games you played in leagues owned by other people stay in those leagues, without your name or account linked. Diagnostic reports are not linked to your account and are deleted automatically after 30 days. Attendance links are deleted 90 days after the planned date. Games kept only on your phone are removed when you delete them or the app.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

You can edit or clear your profile, remove a league from online sharing (Unlink league), withdraw a player claim, delete games and players in the app, and turn off automatic error reports in Settings › Backup & data › Diagnostics.

Support

Restore Padelitics Pro

Settings › Padelitics Pro › Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you subscribed with.

Redeem a code

Tap "Have a code?" on the paywall, or Settings › Redeem a code.

Cancel a subscription

iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Padelitics. Android: Play Store › Profile › Payments & subscriptions.

Move to a new phone

Settings › Back up, then restore that file on the new phone. Padelitics Pro moves with your store account.

Contact

Email dev@gripfitnessstudio.com. We usually reply within two working days.
